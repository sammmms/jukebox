Party Jukebox Release Notes

Current version: v2.4.2

v2.4.2 (Latest) Nov 2026
  • 🎨 Elegant Dialog Modal Padding: Restored spacious padding (24-32px), glowing centered icon, high-contrast typography, and responsive rounded dialog cards.
  • 🎵 Fixed Mandarin Recommendation Tracks: Corrected official video IDs for Eric Chou's 怎么了 (What's Wrong) and Firdhaus' 阿拉斯加海湾 (Gulf of Alaska) with instant 1-tap queueing and reliable thumbnails.
  • 🛡️ Refined Catalog Validator: Fixed false-positive 429 rate-limit rejections, ensuring all valid YouTube Music songs add seamlessly from search or recommendations.
v2.4.1 Nov 2026
  • 🚀 Auto-Update Version Watchdog: Real-time detection of server updates with an automated floating countdown banner that seamlessly reloads open tabs without manual effort.
  • 🧲 10% Volume Snapping: Quick 1-tap snap toggle (0%, 10%, 20%... 100%) beside the volume slider with local preference persistence.
  • 🛡️ YouTube Music Track Validation: Rejects unplayable/non-music YouTube links and prevents previous song audio hang.
v2.3.0 Nov 2026
  • 👑 Auto-Vacant Host Architecture: Strict separation between Party Host (room master controller, read-only for guests) and Guest Requester Profiles. If the Host disconnects or is offline, the position automatically becomes Vacant so another attendee can claim Host.
  • ✨ Reactive Interactive Category Chips: Tapping any category recommendation chip immediately displays active vibrant state, triggers instantaneous filtered search, displays a results counter banner, and allows 1-tap toggling.
  • 🌐 Cloudflare Public Domain: Permanent public endpoint at https://dj.wsnsam.my.id bypassing DHCP resets and CGNAT completely.
v2.2.0 Nov 2026
  • 🎧 Dynamic DJ System: Removed hardcoded DJ profiles. Any party guest can enter their custom requester nickname.
  • 🚀 Mandatory First-Time Onboarding: First-time visitors are welcomed and prompted to choose their requester profile.
  • 🎨 Deterministic Dynamic Color Palette: Generates beautiful matching color pills, badges, and avatar dots.
v2.1.0 Nov 2026
  • 🇨🇳 Mandarin & C-Pop Ecosystem: Added dedicated China & Taiwan country filters, Mandopop / KTV chips, and curated recommendations (Jay Chou, JJ Lin, GEM, etc.).
  • 🧩 Modern Custom Components: Glassmorphic custom select dropdowns, search input with clear button & / hotkey, glowing timeline playhead, and dynamic volume controls.
  • 🎯 Isolated Lyrics Scroll: Auto-scroll only triggers when fullscreen lyrics modal is focused; outer page never jumps.
  • 🔄 Reliable Refresh Engine: Tactile animated spinning refresh with cache-busting headers (no-store) and automatic SSE reconnection.
v2.0.0 Nov 2026
  • Widescreen Desktop Dashboard: 2-column layout with embedded lyrics and Host Wi-Fi QR Card.
  • View Modes Switcher: Instant switching between ⚡ Auto, 📱 Mobile, and 💻 Desktop.
  • System Theme Matching: Automatic day/night sync with macOS/iOS (🌓 System, ☀️ Light, 🌙 Dark).
v1.2.0 Nov 2026
  • Real-Time Karaoke Lyrics: Synced LRC lyrics streamed from LRCLib API.
  • COPPA Watchdog: Auto-unpauses "Made for Kids" YouTube videos automatically.
  • Up Next Preview: Shows the next upcoming track with 1-tap play now.
v1.1.0 & v1.0.0 Nov 2026
  • Drag-and-drop queue reordering (SortableJS), crowd upvoting (🔥), previously played history, physical master speaker volume control, and initial SSE engine.
